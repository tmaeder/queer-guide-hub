# Decision-ready review cases and automatic evidence research

The user approved continuing the measured backlog solutions: eliminate repeated
work, research missing evidence autonomously, and present the complete choice on
the decision page. Preserve the existing interface system and raw review audit.

Consolidate venue linkage by event, missing settlement identity/lifecycle by city,
and shared artwork by its evidence group. Other workflows retain their native
actions, source permissions, undo capability and audit records.

Display Ready, Research and Blocked populations explicitly, with the raw task
count and inclusive detector scope still reachable. A research job is not a
resolved finding. Missing candidates and missing identity evidence enter durable
jobs with evidence fingerprints, leases, three attempts and backoff. Changed
source evidence or venue catalogue facts wake the job; exhausted jobs expose the
reason and can be retried explicitly. Keep pause switches effective.

The worker reuses the existing guarded city identity resolver and collects
Wikidata name, country, coordinates and settlement classes. It never promotes an
identity from a name-only match, changes city indexing, or infers a business
ownership relationship. For venue cases it searches canonical same-city/country
records, automatically links only a unique exact name within 500 metres when
both coordinates exist, and refuses ambiguous exact-name sets and occurrence
collisions. Weaker matches become proposals. Bounded publisher-page retrieval
extracts only structured event facts matching the current event's title; every
redirect passes the existing URL guard.

Mechanical repairs recompute canonical active product counts. A neutral event
category with no proposed subtype receives an explicit machine acceptance of
that limitation, retaining its detector evidence/hash and never claiming a
verified subtype or human decision. Actual repairs have before/after audit rows.

every member. Selection supports per-finding exceptions. Acceptance requires a
note and confirmation; the server checks all selected live evidence before
applying the native per-source actions in one transaction. Venue decisions
recheck the current event version, canonical venue, geography and occurrence
