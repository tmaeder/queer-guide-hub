# Explicit evidence for missing registered articles

The old social-nudity import registered Wikipedia links as its sole source for many venue profiles. In a fresh sample of 100 editorial findings, the official API reported 54 mainspace articles missing, 43 existing articles and three disambiguation pages. Repeatedly requesting business prose, contacts or media from a missing article cannot repair the profile. An absent article is not evidence that the physical place does not exist.

The collector defaults to preview. Collection binds each exact HTTPS mainspace URL to its stable registered source ID, checks the official API in batches of 20 with no redirects and no cache allowance, and retains selected page JSON, full-response and selected-page hashes and actual observation times. Namespace links, interwiki links, sections, title mismatches, contradictory IDs, transport failures and unknown responses cannot establish absence. Present and disambiguation pages are recorded as present; neither supplies identity or prose.

A private append-only observation table retains this evidence against the original source UUID, URL and payload hash. The internal registration RPC defaults to dry run, validates fresh captures, locks source and profile rows and preserves all original source, venue and organization facts. It invalidates the quality cursor only when the source-availability contract changes. Journals are persisted before writes; unknown outcomes must be reconciled before retrying.

Ordinary business quality reassessment recognizes only a sole, matching registered Wikipedia source from this particular importer with explicit missing-page evidence and no supplied prose, contact or media facts. Claims, verification, human changes, waivers, unknown field ownership, additional sources and additional locations remain protected. New source evidence, a changed source hash or a later present result invalidates the missing-source contract. Existing values retain their normal assessment; no score or finding is marked passed by the observation writer.

Validation: seven parser tests, scoped lint and 35 isolated PostgreSQL cases, including source drift, a later present observation, human ownership, supplied facts, Unicode and literal-plus titles, fresh-proof validation, access controls, idempotence and unchanged full rows. The immutable migration and operator have not yet been applied to production.
