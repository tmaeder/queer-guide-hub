# Meetups: colourful social invitations

Approved by the user on 2026-11-25: “Colourful social invitations — warm, playful and easy to use”.

Keep the consolidated Meet structure and the existing invitation, consent, authentication, safety and Messages flows. Limit this visual amplification to Meetups; People, Groups and Dating retain their quieter frames.

- Give the opening expressive Anton typography and a confident pink-and-ink surface, using the existing Meet accent and brand tokens. Do not reassign identity track colours to activities.
- Replace the activity dropdown with directly selectable, tactile activity choices. A compact horizontal strip on phones keeps the city/date/search task in reach. Labels remain visible and every choice has a keyboard focus state and selected state.
- Present actual invitations as generous social cards: the activity, title, date/place, host avatar/name and one joining action. Never manufacture hosts, invitations, attendance or imagery to fill a quiet community.
- Keep secondary group opportunities quieter, and preserve distinct initial, loading, empty, signed-out and error states. Short, friendly copy and real content supply warmth.
- Motion belongs to the activity interaction and respects reduced motion. Preserve dark-mode contrast, 44px controls, locale expansion and document overflow boundaries.

Reuse the shared page frame through an optional opening slot. Keep activity selection presentation separate from discovery state. Validate the desktop/mobile opening, active activity filtering, invitation consent and authentication continuity, plus existing route/locale checks.
