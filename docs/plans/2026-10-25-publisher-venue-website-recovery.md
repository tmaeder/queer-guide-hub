# Recover venue relationships from publisher and official-site maps

The unlinked-venue cohort includes 161 Siegessäule events. Its detail pages publish a typed venue LocationPoint in their Sapper data, but the importer discarded it. Native events consequently retained city-centroid estimates and the existing matcher could not corroborate venue aliases reliably.

The parser now retains one distinct valid typed LocationPoint without evaluating scripts. It refuses competing, missing, out-of-range and zero-pair points. The importer records these coordinates and the venue homepage in source metadata for guarded matching; pin ownership stays with the resolver.

Read-only research found a source-confirmed subset of 24 Insomnia occurrences. Each current publisher page names Insomnia, links its official homepage and supplies a point consistent with the existing canonical venue. The independently checked official contact page publishes the same address and its own map point. The collector preserves all 25 full source captures, SHA-256 hashes and timestamps. It checks exact occurrence URL/time/title bindings, homepage host/path identity and agreement of both source points with the canonical venue within 100 metres. Official capture adapters are explicitly scoped to the independently checked host.

Sources:
- Publisher occurrence: https://www.siegessaeule.de/termine/kultur/kinky-berlin-tour-4/2026-10-03/16:30/
- Official contact and map: https://www.insomnia-berlin.de/kontakt.html
