# Bounded corroborated contact captures

The admin/internal enrichment preview has a separate evidence mode for up to five native venue identities. It reads the current names, coordinates and native country/city identities, searches within 100 m, and captures only Pro fields with the existing configured credential. It does not import or stage places, write contacts, or infer that missing results mean absent contacts. Captures retain the exact response, SHA-256, query URL, API version and observation time. Bodies are capped at 1 MB and requests time out after 15 seconds. A full 50-result window cannot establish uniqueness and remains unresolved.

A positive match requires a unique name/country match within 60 m and compatible street-address tokens. An addressless native record needs 30 m. The existing native city name may qualify an otherwise identical business name; other name differences remain unresolved. Closed/disputed results, invalid identities, unsafe URLs and unsuitable phone strings are excluded. Cruising, toilet and unresolved-category venues are not collected through this business-contact path.

The native `source-foursquare-free` deployment contains an existing free-tier budget helper absent from the checkout. Its helper is recovered unchanged and reused before the preview or evidence mode makes any API calls. The ledger retains its 500 monthly call limit and 50-call safety margin, and fails closed on accounting errors. The initial pilot did not use that helper; collection was paused and its 81 submitted calls, including the preview and in-flight batch, were conservatively recorded under `contact-evidence:pilot-accounting`. The existing separate source had used one call: the ledger then showed 82 spent and 368 remaining. No limit, billing configuration or credential was changed.

`collect-foursquare-contact-evidence.mjs` defaults to a no-call preview and caps an explicit collection run at 100 identities. It records native HTTP receipts before waiting and stops on unknown outcomes, missing evidence mode or source failures. Completed captures can be retained across ordinary deployments; unavailable functions do not become negative evidence.

The default-dry SQL registration operator recomputes the unique named/country/coordinate/address match from the retained response, requires a fresh successful website probe and unchanged native records, and excludes human ownership, claims, existing Foursquare identity, source conflicts and known editorial/media source limitations. It records the real source and only fills empty venue contacts. Original source captures remain available; native triggers populate the Foursquare identity and canonical phone bookkeeping. Whole-row assertions permit exactly those derived values. The complete organization remains unchanged during registration; its ordinary source inheritance producer owns contact reassessment.

The first guarded production run registered three records: Alte Kaserne Winterthur, 8-Bar and 25hours Hotel Langstrasse. Their websites returned HTTP 200 with matching brand titles. Production rollback assertions passed before committing. The existing native business source reconciler subsequently filled three business websites and corrected three contact findings. The fourth positive place capture was excluded by the stricter registration guards. Fifteen provider, identity and budget regressions pass, along with edge checking and scoped lint. Other captures and pending counts are not credited as repairs.
