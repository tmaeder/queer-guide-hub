# Admin taxonomy organization

Requested: consolidate the cluttered settings page under Tags and Governance.

The canonical Tags workspace reuses the CMS list and record editor. Categories & tools contains CSV import, AI creation, geo linking, bulk descriptions and categorization. Vocabularies links directly to the seven controlled vocabulary lists. Legal sources joins aliases and diagnostic codes in the record editor. Export, creation and editing reuse the CMS controls.

Governance gains a Tags mode with individually disclosed sensitive review, enrichment suggestions, quality, vocabulary health and hygiene panels. Assignment suggestions remain in the existing triage queue; tag and vocabulary merges remain in the existing Merge surface, reached by a URL-backed taxonomy family selection.

The settings entry disappears from navigation and redirects to Tags. Existing settings vocabulary redirects remain valid. Editor access to tag records remains unchanged; moved collection tools and vocabulary links require moderator, and governance and vocabulary route floors remain moderator.

Alternatives considered: leaving shortcuts on Settings would retain a third overlapping destination; putting all old panels above the CMS list would move the clutter. This organization gives each task one home and defers data requests until its section opens.
