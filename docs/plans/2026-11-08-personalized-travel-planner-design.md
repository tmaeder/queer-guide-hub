# Personalized travel planner

Replace the template rail on `/travel` with a single-destination trip planner. Travelers can preview a day-by-day itinerary before signing in. Use their destination, dates, adult party size, budget, pace and interests to build it. Signed-in travelers can use their saved travel and accessibility preferences and can resume the trip they were working on.

Build suggestions from published Queer Guide venues and events. Show country rights information with the preview and leave gaps visible where the guide has limited coverage. Let travelers keep or remove suggestions before saving, then edit and reorder the saved itinerary in the trip workspace.

Save the reviewed itinerary and day assignments together. Carry dates, destination and adult party size into the existing flight, stay and activity booking surfaces where the partner supports those inputs. Travelers pay with the partner. Queer Guide tracks offers as pending and lets travelers paste or forward confirmations, review the imported details and attach them to the trip.

A guest should be able to preview and edit a plan, sign in, recover that preview and save it to one private trip. A returning traveler should be able to reach their itinerary, bookings, rights briefing and confirmation import from the same trip. The main travel page keeps its booking deep links and public-trip discovery.
