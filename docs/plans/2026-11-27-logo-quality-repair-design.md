# Logo repair and prevention

Approved by the user on 2026-11-27, including live repair and review of uncertain replacements.

Prefer the entity's declared website logo over a domain-logo provider. Unwrap Google Translate links, refuse platform/parking identities, and retain valid customer assets hosted by CMS CDNs. Require measured raster dimensions (at least 256px on the long edge and 48px on the short edge), safe SVGs, and corroboration of the entity's identity. Provider and icon-only candidates need review; never manufacture detail by enlarging a thumbnail.

Audit existing venue, event, organization, and marketplace brand logos through a durable, retryable worklist. Automatically publish only well-supported official assets. Quarantine known platform/CMS marks, preserve prior values and evidence, and send uncertain cases into the existing general review queue. Use optimistic concurrency checks when publishing so an editor's intervening changes win.

Prevent recurrence with database write guards, shared enrichment checks, provenance, and periodic worklist processing. Keep logo assets separate from cover photos. Record repair totals and remaining review work after live verification.
