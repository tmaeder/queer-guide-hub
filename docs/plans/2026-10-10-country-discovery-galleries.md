# Country discovery galleries

The user requested a more playful country page with city pictures and advanced, photo-based venue and event galleries. This extends the approved cities-and-travel-first subway design and retains the photo album and polished rights section.

- City links become photo cards with familiar route bullets and a small directional hover movement. Photos stay in full colour; labels stay on paper.
- Venue and event galleries are visible sections. Search, city and category remain immediately available; additional filters open within the toolbar.
- Venue filters include price and verification. Event filters include a date range and free entry. Both offer sorting, reset, honest totals and pagination.
- Filtering happens on the server over the country collection before pagination. Known city suggestions use stable IDs; other typed cities remain searchable within the same country.
- Archived, merged and closed venues remain excluded. Active events preserve ongoing-event overlap and recurring-series grouping outside explicit date ranges.
- Missing photos use entity icons. A venue logo can serve as a contained fallback. Loading, errors, empty results, invalid date ranges and keyboard focus are explicit.
- Country safety warnings, promotion gates, source attribution, legal values and map navigation retain their existing behavior. Rights stay monochrome.

Verify country scoping, filter composition, pagination and event overlap with meaningful tests; check desktop, mobile, German copy and accessibility against real data before the authorized deployment.
