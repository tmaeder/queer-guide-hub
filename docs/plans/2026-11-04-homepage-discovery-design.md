# Homepage discovery

The homepage helps first-time visitors find queer places and events. The first screen gives search and direct routes to venues, events, and the map. The guide’s display type, track colors, and transit icons carry its identity. The links follow normal reading and focus order in left-to-right and right-to-left layouts.

The rest of the page offers four listings, four city guides, and up to three recently viewed links. Travel, people, rights, news, history, the glossary, and marketplace have direct links in a compact directory. The existing support actions close the page.

The regional sections share the existing location preference and permit manual correction. While region or data is loading they show placeholders. The listing heading reflects the scope of the results, and events beyond that scope are grouped separately. Initial query failures offer retry in place. Refresh failures retain existing results. If regional context fails to render, the sections can load globally.

Verify at desktop and phone widths, including a narrow right-to-left viewport. Check search submission, localized route navigation, region and refresh states, recent-history behavior, and visibility of the secondary destinations.
