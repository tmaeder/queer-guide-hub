# City-led discovery pilot

Travel and community have equal weight. A visitor can begin with a city, find something to do there, and discover a public way to connect. Search and directory access stay in the opening view for visitors arriving with a specific task.

## First slice

Feature Zürich in the homepage opening, with its existing city photograph and transit network as a fallback. Link to the city guide, a dated public event from the existing city-scoped feed, and Regenbogenhaus. Link to the Zürich event directory when there is no eligible event. The city guide and venue detail already provide paths into trip planning. Use the existing product design tokens, localized navigation, and content visibility policies.

## Follow-through

City guides bring the photo and up to two local listings into the opening overview, with the safety verdict first and the map and practical details below. Events leads with a near-term listing from the current feed during default browsing. Shared filters, search, timeline, and map views go straight to their results and controls.

Meet people opens with local gatherings and community spaces when available, plus a public group. The connection map has a shortcut in the opening and loads when approached. Travel opens with the published cover or a seasonal destination and its reason to go. An active trip gets a direct continuation link, and booking links open the booking section.

The Venues directory pairs its title with a real featured place during default browsing and puts results before guides and recommendations. Venue pages open with the description and available photo, with shortcuts to location and hours and a dated event when one is available.

Going out opens with a dated event and a place to visit when listings are available. Events outside the selected city name their location, and the opening gives a local venue priority when the event is elsewhere.

Test whether visitors can find one thing they would do and one way to connect. Compare that experience with time to reach search, event and venue detail opens, and actions that carry an idea into a trip. Review at mobile and desktop widths, with missing imagery, sparse listings, and signed-in travel context.

Community Groups opens with recently active public groups and the next public group meetup, when one is listed in the next 30 days. Group and event details, browsing, signing in, and group creation are reachable from the opening.

Group details lead with the group’s description, the next meetup, and the action that fits the visitor’s membership. Signed-out visitors can preview public groups and their eligible meetups. Private meetup details are reserved for members.

## Public discovery follow-through

Cities opens with links to destinations that have upcoming events. Country and village guides bring visit ideas forward after the safety verdict. Stays places a featured hotel in its opening and keeps the directory above the inspiration rails. Organizations introduces active support and advocacy organizations. Hotel and organization profiles bring their main action closer to the title. Event details show available artwork alongside the essential decision information. The default Pride and Personalities pages lead with upcoming celebrations and featured people. The glossary introduces readers to real definitions before the taxonomy. Personality profiles show the beginning of the biography, the News archive puts the featured story before category controls, a quiet Hub points members to groups and events, and Shop departments introduce an available buying guide. The Guides index leads with a featured guide, and guide detail has a shortcut to its recommendations. Competition category pages put featured winners before counts where the archive has portraits.

Knowledge details open with the definition or key facts. Glossary entries point to relevant reference guides, sources, or the topic directory. History entries and the rights and health reference pages offer direct links to the story, sources, and practical sections. Personality profiles link to biographies and related records when available. The remaining audit covers lower-traffic public detail and account routes. News already leads with a story, and History already leads with dated milestones. Search, maps, and filtered views should give the visitor their requested results first. Account, admin, editing, and booking flows need clear task order and compact controls.

Podcast show pages bring the newest published episode forward, with its description and the existing player. Show identity precedes artwork on phones. Wishlists open with saved lists and clear visibility labels, with list creation below and linked from the opening. Shared wishlist pages offer a direct path to the items. Group invitations introduce the group before membership and inviter details, followed by the existing join action.
