# Transdermal Testosterone

Candidate English draft · not approved or published.

Testosterone delivered through the skin, for example in a gel preparation, rather than by injection.


## A route of administration

Transdermal describes how a medicine enters the body. It does not name a separate hormone or a different gender identity. Testosterone products used through the skin can be part of masculinising care or treatment for other clinical indications.


## Product instructions matter

Preparations differ in concentration, application sites and handling instructions. Instructions for one product should not be assumed to apply to another. The relevant prescribing information and a clinician or pharmacist are the appropriate sources for product-specific questions.


## Transfer is a practical concern

Some topical testosterone products can transfer through contact with the application area. Product guidance addresses precautions to limit exposure to other people. A general article should make that issue visible without inventing a universal waiting time or a single application rule for all products.


## Choosing a formulation

Preferences about needles, daily routines, skin contact and access can be part of a discussion about formulation. The question is which option fits the person’s treatment goals and circumstances, not which route represents a more complete transition. Do not switch products or dosing based on a glossary entry.
