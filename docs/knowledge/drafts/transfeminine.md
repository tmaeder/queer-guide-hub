# Transfeminine

Candidate English draft · not approved or published.

An umbrella term used by some trans people whose gender or transition has a feminine direction, including some women and nonbinary people.


## What the umbrella includes

Transfeminine is a broad descriptor rather than a single gender. A person may use it alongside woman, nonbinary, genderqueer or another identity. Some people find it helpful for describing a shared direction of experience while keeping a more specific word for their own gender. Others do not use it at all.


## A direction, not a checklist

The term does not mean that someone must dress in a particular way or pursue hormones, surgery or another medical intervention. Gender identity, expression and medical choices are different matters. There is no required sequence of steps that makes a person’s identity valid.


## Different from feminine

Feminine describes qualities or forms of expression that a culture associates with femininity. People of many genders can be feminine. Transfeminine has a more specific use in trans communities; it should not be assigned to someone simply because of their clothes, voice or gestures.


## Using it with care

Follow the words a person chooses. A transfeminine person who describes herself as a woman is a woman, not an approximation of one. A nonbinary person using the same umbrella may mean something different. If you are explaining a service or support group, describe whom it serves clearly instead of assuming the umbrella has identical boundaries for everyone.
