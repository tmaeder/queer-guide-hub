# Cisgender

Candidate English draft · not approved or published.

Describing a person whose gender aligns with the sex they were assigned at birth.


## A word for a relationship

Cisgender names the relationship between gender and sex assignment. It is usually shortened to cis after its meaning is clear. It does not describe someone’s sexual orientation: cis people can be lesbian, gay, bisexual, straight, asexual or use other terms.


## Identity and appearance

Clothing, voice, hairstyle and mannerisms do not establish whether a person is cisgender. A cis woman may dress in a masculine way; a cis man may enjoy feminine clothing. Gender expression is one part of how someone moves through the world, not a reliable way to identify their history or gender.


## Using the term

Use cisgender as an adjective, as in “cisgender people”. In a discussion of services for trans and cis people, it can make the comparison clear without treating one group as the unnamed default. In other situations, the distinction may be irrelevant. Someone introducing a colleague usually does not need to mention it.


## What the word does not tell you

Knowing that someone is cis does not tell you their anatomy, fertility, personality, politics or relationship to conventional gender roles. It also does not mean their experience is shared by every other cis person. The term is useful when it makes a relevant distinction; it should not replace a person’s own account of their life.
