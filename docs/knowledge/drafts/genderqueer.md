# Genderqueer

Candidate English draft · not approved or published.

A self-description for gender that questions or moves beyond conventional divisions between woman and man.


## An identity with room in it

Genderqueer can describe a gender outside a binary, a relationship to more than one gender, or a deliberate challenge to expectations about gender. People do not all use it in the same way. A short definition can offer a starting point, but it cannot settle what the word means for a particular person.


## Related, without being interchangeable

Genderqueer and nonbinary overlap. Some people use both; others choose one because of its personal, cultural or political associations. Neither word supplies a complete account of someone’s experience. Transgender may also be relevant, but a glossary should not require someone to adopt every available umbrella term.


## Expression is a separate question

There is no required genderqueer appearance. Clothes and presentation can be a way of expressing gender, experimenting, enjoying a style or simply getting dressed. Someone does not have to look androgynous to use this identity. Pronouns also need to be learned from the person rather than inferred from the label.


## In conversation

Use genderqueer when someone uses it for themself. If you need to know how to address them, ask the relevant question directly and respectfully. Learning a person’s name or pronouns does not require asking about their body, medical history or previous identity.
