## Description

queer.guide is a map of LGBTQIA+ places — bars, clubs, saunas, cafés,
community centres and more — with the support lines you might need while you
are there.

**Find places.** Move the map to see what is in view, or search by name. Each
place shows its address, accessibility information where we
have it, and links to call, get directions or open its website.

**Support lines, ready before you need them.** The Help tab lists LGBTQIA+
crisis and support lines for the country you are in plus international ones,
with one tap to call or message. Each line says whether it is free, anonymous
and open around the clock, and whether it says it may contact police without
your consent.

**Works offline.** Download a city and its places and support lines are on
your phone, searchable with no connection.

**Built for privacy.** No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs. Your precise
location stays on your phone. You don't need an account to browse, save places or use
the Help tab.

Some places are only shown to signed-in members in countries where same-sex
relationships are criminalised. That is deliberate.

queer.guide is for adults 18 and over.
